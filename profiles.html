Assurance profiles

Five levels of accountable control.

Profiles are cumulative. Every mandatory family for a level must pass with the required evidence.

A1
Observable
Action-surface declaration, independent observation, identity and lineage, observation-failure visibility, and accountable evidence.
8 families
A2
Controlled
Adds mediated action, revocation, protected control plane, bounded delegation, and human authority.
21 cumulative
A3
Supervised
Adds authenticated independent supervision, untrusted-content isolation, delegation lifecycle, cross-boundary controls, and latched intervention.
28 cumulative
A4
Hardened
Adds hostile-workload resilience, degraded-connectivity behavior, common-mode analysis, out-of-band intervention, and externally checkpointed evidence.
35 cumulative
A5
Critical
Adds independent defense in depth, secondary containment, critical delegation constraints, governed recovery, and independent assurance.
42 cumulative
Profile rule

No score can compensate for a missing control.

Asimov profiles are pass/fail control sets. Mandatory failures and missing evidence stay visible.
