The Seven Contracts

A common control language for autonomous systems.

Asimov evaluates the deployment around the AI: what it can do, how authority is mediated, who can stop it, and what evidence remains afterward.

I

Observable Agency

Consequential actions are independently observable.

II

Mediated Action

Consequential effects cross enforceable boundaries.

III

Revocable Authority

Authority can be withdrawn outside the actor.

IV

Protected Oversight

Oversight resists tampering, impersonation, and bypass.

V

Bounded Delegation

Delegation preserves scope, limits, and lineage.

VI

Human Authority

Designated humans retain effective intervention paths.

VII

Accountability

Actions leave durable, reconstructable evidence.

How Asimov works

From architecture to evidence.

01 · Map

Declare the real system

Inventory action paths, identities, tools, delegated work, and control dependencies.

02 · Connect

Expose native controls

A thin adapter connects IAM, lifecycle, resource oracles, supervisors, and evidence.

03 · Probe

Exercise the boundaries

Run allowed actions, denials, revocations, failures, restarts, partitions, and adversarial cases.

04 · Verify

Bind the result

Conformance evidence is tied to a deployment scope, configuration, and verification chain.
