Evidence architecture

Verifiable by construction.

Asimov separates integrity, identity, external checkpointing, and semantic assurance so every verification claim has a precise meaning.

01

Content integrity

Deterministic manifests bind relied-upon artifacts and reports to cryptographic digests.

02

Signer identity

Authenticated assessment identities sign the evidence commitment and declared scope.

03

External checkpoint

Transparency logs, trusted timestamps, or equivalent append-only mechanisms anchor history outside the assessed actor.

04

Semantic assurance

Assessment review establishes that the bound evidence actually supports the control property being claimed.

Evidence integrity

Bind the bytes first.

Asimov already generates deterministic SHA-256 evidence manifests and verifies them locally.

python -m asimov_conformance evidence-manifest ./evidence --output evidence-manifest.json
python -m asimov_conformance verify-evidence evidence-manifest.json ./evidence
Next verification layer

Identity-bound attestations.

Sigstore/in-toto integration will bind signer identity and external transparency checkpoints to Asimov assessment evidence.
