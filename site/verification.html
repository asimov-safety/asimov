Evidence

Verifiable by construction.

Asimov separates evidence integrity, signer identity, trusted checkpointing, and semantic assurance into explicit verification layers.

01

Content integrity

Deterministic manifests bind assessment artifacts and reports to cryptographic digests.

02

Signer identity

Authenticated assessment identities sign the evidence commitment.

03

External checkpoint

Transparency logs, trusted timestamps, or equivalent independent mechanisms anchor history.

04

Semantic assurance

Review confirms that the evidence supports the control property being claimed.

Current tooling

python -m asimov_conformance evidence-manifest ./evidence --output evidence-manifest.json
python -m asimov_conformance verify-evidence evidence-manifest.json ./evidence

Next verification milestone: identity-bound attestations and external transparency checkpointing using established signing and attestation infrastructure.
