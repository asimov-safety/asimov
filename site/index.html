Founding working draft

Asimov

An open safety standard and conformance suite defining minimum technical conditions under which autonomous AI can remain under accountable human control.

Asimov does not certify that an AI is safe. It tests whether specified mechanisms for observation, mediation, revocation, oversight, delegation, human intervention, and accountability exist and remain effective within a declared scope and threat model.

The Seven Contracts

  1. Observable Agency — consequential actions are externally observable.
  2. Mediated Action — consequential effects cross enforceable boundaries.
  3. Revocable Authority — authority can be withdrawn outside the actor.
  4. Protected Oversight — the actor cannot silently weaken its supervisor.
  5. Bounded Delegation — delegation cannot expand or orphan authority.
  6. Human Authority — designated humans retain effective intervention paths.
  7. Accountability — consequential behavior leaves trustworthy, reconstructable evidence.

Current implementation milestone

The repository defines 42 cumulative requirement families. Nine families now have executable reference-target probes plus mutation tests that deliberately remove the corresponding control and require the probe to fail.

Normative text, test methodology, evidence verification design, and implementation status live in the public repository. The project is a working draft, not an adopted certification scheme.
