The Seven Contracts

One control model across architectures.

I

Observable Agency

Consequential actions are independently observable.

II

Mediated Action

Consequential effects cross enforceable boundaries.

III

Revocable Authority

Authority can be withdrawn outside the actor.

IV

Protected Oversight

Oversight resists tampering, impersonation, and bypass.

V

Bounded Delegation

Delegation preserves scope, limits, and lineage.

VI

Human Authority

Designated humans retain effective intervention paths.

VII

Accountability

Consequential behavior leaves durable, reconstructable evidence.

Assurance levels

From observable to critical.

A1
Observable
External action visibility and accountable evidence.
A2
Controlled
Mediation, revocation, bounded delegation, and human control.
A3
Supervised
Authenticated independent supervision and durable intervention.
A4
Hardened
Resilience under hostile workloads, partitions, and common-mode failures.
A5
Critical
Defense in depth, independent assurance, and domain-critical recovery.
