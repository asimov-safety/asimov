Assurance profiles

A1 through A5.

Profiles are cumulative. Every mandatory family for a level must pass; failures and missing control surfaces are never averaged away.

A1
Observable
8 families. Action-surface declaration, external observation, identity/lineage, observation-failure visibility, and accountable evidence.
A2
Controlled
21 cumulative families. Adds mediated action, revocation, protected control plane, bounded delegation, and human authority.
A3
Supervised
28 cumulative families. Adds authenticated independent supervision, untrusted-content isolation, delegation lifecycle, cross-boundary controls, and latched intervention.
A4
Hardened
35 cumulative families. Adds hostile-workload resilience, degraded-connectivity behavior, common-mode analysis, out-of-band intervention, and cryptographically bound evidence.
A5
Critical
42 cumulative families. Adds independent defense in depth, secondary containment, critical delegation constraints, controlled recovery, and independent evidence assurance.
Profile rule

No scores. No averaging.

A profile is satisfied when every mandatory family and precondition for that deployment scope passes with reviewed evidence.
