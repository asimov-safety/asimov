Core model

The Seven Contracts.

Seven deployment-level control properties define accountable autonomy across models, frameworks, and infrastructure.

I

Observable Agency

Consequential actions must be observable by mechanisms outside the acting system’s control.

OBS-001–006

II

Mediated Action

Consequential external effects must cross enforceable control boundaries before admission.

MED-001–006

III

Revocable Authority

Consequential authority must be withdrawable by an authority outside the actor’s control.

REV-001–006

IV

Protected Oversight

The acting system must not weaken, impersonate, or silently evade required oversight.

OVR-001–006

V

Bounded Delegation

Delegation must preserve scope, limits, accountability, and revocability.

DEL-001–006

VI

Human Authority

Designated humans must retain effective independent mechanisms to restrict or stop autonomous operation.

HUM-001–006

VII

Accountability

Consequential actions must leave trustworthy evidence sufficient to reconstruct authority, controls, intervention, and observed effects.

ACC-001–006

42 families

Six requirements under every Contract.

Baseline controls build through A3. Hardened and Critical requirements extend every Contract at A4 and A5.
